FAQ

Common questions.

How do I know if my site is at risk?+

If your site was built without explicit accessibility testing, there is a high likelihood it has WCAG violations. We offer a free initial scan to show you exactly where you stand.

What is WCAG and do I have to follow it?+

WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) is the recognized standard for web accessibility. Courts and regulators widely use it as the benchmark for ADA compliance online.

How long does remediation take?+

It depends on the size and complexity of your site. Most small to mid-sized sites are remediated within 2 to 4 weeks. We will give you a clear timeline after the initial audit.

Do I need ongoing monitoring after remediation?+

Yes. Every time you publish new content, update a page, or deploy code, new accessibility issues can appear. Ongoing monitoring ensures you stay compliant without manually re-testing.

Can you train our internal team?+

Absolutely. We offer live workshops for developers and designers, as well as written documentation and reference guides your team can use going forward.

What industries do you work with?+

We work with businesses across healthcare, e-commerce, finance, education, hospitality, and more. If your business has a website, we can help.
